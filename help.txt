NAME
        qwx - The extended data walk workspace

DESCRIPTION

        qwx is an ultra-fast, modal terminal application designed for developers, system admins,
        teams looking to optimize their workflow, without ever leaving the command line.

        Developed in Rust, it eliminates the need for heavy graphical IDEs by consolidating code
        editing, server management, and team collaboration into a single, cohesive, and
        lightning-fast text-based interface.

PHILOSOPHY

        To master qwx, you must embrace its core philosophy:

                1. Keep your hands on the keyboard's home row.
                2. No mouse, no popup, no context switching, no friction.
                3. Built for speed.
                4. A structured visual grid system for instant navigation between workspaces.
                5. A single tool to replace your current ones.
                6. A visual grid system to streamline your workflow.
                7. Fusionable panels to mold the interface to your needs.
                8. Maintain deep focus.

NAVIGATION

        KEYBOARD

                KEYS

                        Ctrl represent a control action.
                        Alt represent a change data action.

        MOVEMENT

                Movement in qwx is modal and spatial, relying on standard home-row mechanics.

                     ^                     The k key is at the top and move top.
                     k              Tips:  The h key is at the left and moves left.
               < h       l >               The l key is at the right and moves right.
                     j                     The j key is at the down and move down.
                     v
        
        CURSOR

                Cursor movement is the atomic unit of navigation in qwx.

                By strictly adhering to the standard home-row bindings, you traversetext, logs,
                and lists with zero friction.

                Your fingers never leave the baseline, ensuring maximum typing speed and efficiency.

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate cursor:

                        The 'h' key moves left.
                        The 'l' key moves right.
                        The 'j' key moves down.
                        The 'k' key moves up.

                A panel..

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate panel:
                
                        Ctrl+h moves focus to the left panel.
                        Ctrl+l moves focus to the right panel.
                        Ctrl+j moves focus to the bottom panel.
                        Ctrl+k moves focus to the top panel.

        VIEW
        
                A View...

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate view:
                
                        Alt+k moves focus to the next view.

        WORKSPACE
        
                A Workspace...

                Workspaces are dedicated groups of tabs contained within a panel.

                Workspace Focus enables horizontal cycling through your opened environments.

                Think of it as paginating through different active projects.

                You can also summon a visual tree to map your topology.

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate workspace:
                
                        Alt+w print a tree to display workspaces infos.
                        Alt+l moves focus to the next workspace.
                        Alt+h moves focus to the prev workspace.
        FACE

                A Face...

                Face represents a high-level layout of your Environment.

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate face:
                
                        Alt+n moves focus to the next face.
                        Alt+p moves focus to the previews face.
        FACET

                A Facet...

                Facets are the two opposing sides of a Face—a recto and a verso.

                The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate facet:

                        Alt+x toggle between the primary facet (A) and the secondary facet (B) of the current Face.
                        Alt+a force display of the facet A (front/recto).
                        Alt+z force display of the facet Z (back/verso).

        MODE

                FUSION

                        Fusion mode is your tool for reshaping your workspace.

                        Designed to adapt to the complexity of your tasks, it allows you to break
                        free from the standard four-panel grid. By merging an active panel with
                        its neighbor, you absorb its space to provide an expanded display area for
                        resource-intensive environments—such as analyzing a long log file or a large
                        Git dashboard.

                        The architecture remains structured, yet the grid geometry instantly adjusts
                        to your needs, without ever requiring a mouse.
                       
                        The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate fusion:
                        
                                Ctrl+f enable the fusion mode and then:

                                        h merges the current panel into the left panel.
                                        l merges the current panel into the right panel.
                                        j merges the current panel into the bottom panel.
                                        k merges the current panel into the top panel.
                                        q redraws the original grid and exit the mode.

                ZEN

                        Zen mode represents the ultimate state of immersion.

                        Unlike merging, this mode neither destroys nor alters the grid,
                        instead, it masks it.

                        Activating it isolates the current panel and centers it on the screen,
                        eliminating visual distractions so you can focus entirely on your code.

                        The true power of Zen mode lies in its "phantom grid":
                        the other panels continue running in the background.

                        Using standard directional keys, you can glide smoothly to another
                        panel—which instantly takes center without ever breaking your concentration.
     
                        The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate fusion:

                                Ctrl+z enable the zen mode and then:
                                
                                        h shifts the Zen focus to the left panel.
                                        l shifts the Zen focus to the right panel.
                                        j shifts the Zen focus to the bottom panel.
                                        k shifts the Zen focus to the top panel.
                                        q redraws the original grid and exit the mode.
                RESCUE

                        Rescue mode...

                        To serves as your safety net for compromised environments.

                        Designed for unstable SSH connections or overloaded servers, this emergency
                        mode instantly disables resource-intensive features—such as continuous
                        syntax analysis, advanced graphical rendering, and secondary network request.

                        Qwx reverts to a pure, raw, and ultra-responsive text-based interface,
                        ensuring you remain in control even under conditions of extreme latency.

                        The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate rescue:

                                Ctrl+r enable the rescue mode.
                BROADCAST

                        Broadcast mode...

                        Brodcast Mode transforms your terminal into a native collaboration space.

                        Leveraging the Sessions architecture, this mode allows multiple developers
                        to connect to the same interface.

                        It synchronizes cursors in real time and manages permissions.

                        It is the ultimate pair-programming experience whithout require an another
                        tool, directly within the SSH session.

                        The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate broadcast:

                                Ctrl+b enable the broadcast mode.
                EPHEMERAL
                
                        Ephemeral mode...

                        Ephemeral Mode is your ultimate, secure scratchpad.

                        It instantly summons a floating layer over your workspace, allowing you
                        to jot down a quick note, format a snippet of code, or isolate a temporary
                        query.

                        Its golden rule is strict: absolutely nothing is stored in memory.
                        As soon as the mode is closed, the buffer is completely and irretrievably
                        destroyed.

                        It is the perfect space for handling volatile data without cluttering
                        your history or leaving any traces.
                        
                        The keyboard shortcuts to manipulate ephemeral:

                                Ctrl+e enable the ephemeral mode.
